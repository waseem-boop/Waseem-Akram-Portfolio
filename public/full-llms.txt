## 4. End-to-End Product Engineering Process

Every project follows an eight-stage methodology that balances strategic clarity with technical execution:

1. Discovery — Your vision, clearly defined.
   Uncovering the core problem, business model, primary goals, target audience, and success metrics.
2. Research — Understand what matters.
   Market and competitor analysis, technical feasibility assessments, and user persona mapping.
3. Strategy — A direction worth following.
   System architecture blueprinting, technology selection, milestone definition, and project roadmap.
4. Design — Ideas become experiences.
   Information architecture, UX wireframes, high-fidelity UI design, and interactive prototype validation.
5. Development — Built with care and clarity.
   Clean, typed, maintainable code implementation across frontend interfaces and backend services.
6. Integration — Everything working together.
   Connecting third-party APIs, webhooks, databases, payment gateways, and authentication layers.
7. Refinement — Test, polish, and perfect.
   Rigorous cross-device testing, edge-case validation, performance profiling, and accessibility auditing.
8. Launch — Ready for your next chapter.
   Zero-downtime deployment, DNS & SSL setup, analytics integration, and structured project handover.
